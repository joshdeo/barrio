The Kitchen

Dishes people
ask for by name.

Pancit for long life. Adobo that disappears first. Kare kare, sisig, lechon kawali, palabok, and a dessert list that runs from turon to biko. The party tray menu covers the dishes every celebration seems to need.

On Dundas Street East in Mississauga, Barrio keeps the kitchen and the cafe side by side.
